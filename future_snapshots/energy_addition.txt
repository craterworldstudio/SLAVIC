🧠 Snapshot: Energy System Integration (Deferred)

The system currently includes an energy variable in state.json, but energy is not yet used to constrain generation or learning.

Energy-based constraints (e.g., scaling rewards, gating generation, or limiting competence growth) are intentionally deferred.

Reason:
At the current stage of development, the model is still stabilizing its word-level structure. Introducing energy-based modulation now would:

Reduce learning signal strength

Slow structural formation

Increase instability during phase consolidation

Mask whether issues are architectural or motivational

Until the learner demonstrates consistent word formation and reduced frustration in the WORDS phase, energy will remain observational only.

Planned future integration:

Energy-modulated reward scaling

Energy-influenced thought frequency

Energy-dependent exploration vs exploitation balance

Recovery cycles affecting learning bursts

For now:
Cognitive development takes priority over vitality simulation.
